Questions, answered

Straight answers for first-time EV buyers.

What range will I really get?

Revolt’s range figures are IDC (claimed) numbers. Real-world range is usually lower and depends on speed, load, road and riding mode. Eco mode and steady riding help you go further.

Can I charge at home or in an apartment?

Yes. The portable charger works on any home socket. The battery is removable, so you can carry it indoors if your parking is far from a plug.

Is the battery removable?

Yes, on all our Revolt bikes.

What is the battery warranty?

8 years or 80,000 km. The battery is replaced if its health falls below 70%. The vehicle has a 5-year or 60,000 km warranty. Terms apply.

Are subsidies available?

The PM E-DRIVE subsidy of up to ₹5,000 may apply on registrations till 31 March 2028, and some states offer their own. Eligibility varies, so please check with us.

Can I get finance or EMI?

Yes. EMIs start from about ₹2,212 a month depending on the bike. We help with finance, and exchange of your old bike is possible.

Which documents do I need?

Typically Aadhaar, PAN, address proof and a passport photo. For finance, bank statements may also be needed. Bring a valid driving licence for your test ride.

How does the test ride work?

Pick a date and time slot in the form above. We confirm by phone, you visit the showroom, and a team member takes you through the bike before you ride.
